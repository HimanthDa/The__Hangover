The Hangover History

How the idea started — and why we built a place to learn and shop.

Where it began

The Hangover started with a simple problem: beverage shopping online was convenient, but it rarely explained what you were buying. Cold drinks and soft drinks often feel “everyday,” and wine can feel “complicated.” We wanted one modern website that treats every drink with the same respect — clear product info, real origins, ingredients, and a story you can trust.

What we believe

  • Transparency — ingredients, origin, and brand details should be easy to understand.
  • Education — wine types, regions, and production shouldn’t be hidden behind jargon.
  • Convenience — browse, filter, add to cart, and checkout in a few clicks.
  • Quality — clean product pages with high‑quality images and consistent information.

Our timeline

  • Idea — build a single platform for cold drinks, soft drinks, and wines.
  • Research — collect beverage histories, ingredient references, and wine education topics.
  • Catalog — create categories and structured product fields (history, origin, ingredients, brand variations).
  • Shop — add cart, checkout, and order history to support online purchasing.
  • Admin — enable product, image, user, and order management through the dashboard.

What’s next

This site is designed to grow: more drinks, more regions, richer timelines, and better recommendations. If you’re exploring wine, check the Wine History section for types, regions, and the winemaking process. For everyday beverages, visit Drink History.
